# Approved MYS Individual Sport Page Template — Version 1

Approved by the site owner on October 7, 2026 (Hawaiʻi time).
Reference: `/sports/canoe-paddling/`, its internal organization-detail pages, and `assets/css/paddling.css` in the v26 complete website.

The approved Canoe Paddling design is the reference template for future individual MYS sport pages. Preserve its visual hierarchy, branded banner treatment, organization cards, in-page navigation, mobile responsiveness and useful internal detail pages. This refinement does not apply the design to any other sport.

## Architecture and principles

- MYS provides useful information directly; external official links support substantial content on MYS.
- Organization profiles contain substantial verified details, including location, age requirements, opportunities, season, registration, public contacts and official links when available.
- Group organizations in ways that help families scan and find a suitable local opportunity.
- Keep community organizations and school programs distinct.
- Keep events separate from permanent organizations, with dated records, internal detail pages and Hawaiʻi-local date handling.
- Include verified instructional, beginner and cultural pathways where the sport has them.
- Handle missing information clearly and naturally. Never invent program details, ages, contacts, schedules, fees or registration availability.
- For an active club without a verified youth offering, use “Contact the club for youth program availability.” This does not establish a youth program.
- For a confirmed youth program without current registration information, use “Contact the club for youth registration information.”
- Welcome families with practical instructions: “Contact the club before visiting to ask about age requirements, registration, and practice schedules.”
- Preserve MauiYouthSports branding, accessibility, mobile usability and completed banner artwork.

## Adaptation, not duplication

Follow the overall architecture and visual design while accommodating sport-specific differences. Do not force every sport into identical sections, island counts, school pathways or seasonal patterns. Include only sections and facts supported by that sport’s evidence. The Canoe Paddling layout is approved; no redesign is authorized by this standard.

## Preservation and review

Retain the shared header, navigation, footer and unrelated site functionality. Check desktop and mobile rendering, internal links, contacts, date-based event behavior and missing assets. Compare the complete site against its starting version and deliver a full-site deployment package; do not publish without the site owner’s instructions.

For this refinement, preserve all Hawaiian names except the explicitly corrected place name: Hoaloha Park.
