Pukalani Skate Jam

One Love Skate and the County of Maui Department of Parks and Recreation host this community skate jam at Pukalani Skatepark on Saturday, Nov. 28, 2026. Registration opens at 2:45 p.m., a beginner lesson runs 3–3:30 p.m., and the jam goes until 6 p.m.

Expect skill-level contests, 45-second runs, Best Trick, food, games and prizes. Helmets are required, pads are recommended, a limited amount of equipment is available to borrow, and every skater needs a completed waiver.

When
Saturday, Nov. 28, 2026, 2:45–6 p.m. HST
Where
Upcountry (Pukalani) Skate Park, 91 Pukalani St.
Who
One Love Skate and the County of Maui Department of Parks and Recreation
