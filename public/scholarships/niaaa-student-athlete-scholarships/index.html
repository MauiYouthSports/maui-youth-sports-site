NIAAA Student-Athlete Scholarships

High-school seniors: B+ average, top quarter of class or qualifying test score; two sports for two years each and a varsity letter in both. Qualifying scores are ACT 24+ or SAT 1100+; satisfy one of the academic alternatives.

2027 nominations are open. Check the sponsor’s site for the Hawaiʻi deadline.

Application timing

The Hawaiʻi deadline is listed as February 28. Check the sponsor’s site to confirm the year.

How to apply / requirements

School athletic director must belong to NIAAA and the state association. Submit the essay/application, director recommendation and certified academic information through the school.

Contact: Michael Ban, Hawaiʻi liaison

Email: michael.ban@k12.hi.us

Requirements may change for future application cycles.
