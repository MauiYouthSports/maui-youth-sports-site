Maui Junior Tennis Grant

Maui residents age 18 or younger demonstrating financial need.

Check the sponsor’s website for application details.
Sponsor
USTA Hawaii Pacific / Maui Community Tennis Organization
Opportunity type
Travel Assistance
Amount
Up to $500
Who can qualify
Maui residents age 18 or younger demonstrating financial need.
Geography
Maui County
Sports
Tennis

Award uses

Travel to approved interisland or mainland tennis tournaments

How to apply / requirements

Parent signs the form. Email only the form to Ron Romano; mail the form and tax documentation. Recipients provide receipts and a one-page benefit summary to MCTO.

Contact: Ron Romano

Email: romano@hawaii.usta.com

Address: 1888 Kalakaua Ave, Suite C309, Honolulu, HI 96815

Contact the sponsor about the details noted above before applying.
