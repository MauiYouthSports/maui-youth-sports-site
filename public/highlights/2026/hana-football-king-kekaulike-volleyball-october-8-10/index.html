Catching up: Hāna wins on Lānaʻi, King Kekaulike volleyball sweeps at Baldwin

Published 2026-10-16

Hāna beat Lānaʻi 36–20 in eight-player football, and King Kekaulike swept Baldwin 3–0 during a seven-match girls volleyball win streak.

Two results from last week didn’t make our earlier roundups, so here they are.

On Saturday, Oct. 10, Hāna beat Lānaʻi 36–20 in an eight-player MIL football game on Lānaʻi. Lānaʻi grabbed a brief 8–6 lead on a long punt return, but Hāna led 12–8 at halftime and outscored the Pine Lads 24–12 after the break. The Dragons have now won two straight to improve to 2–1, while Lānaʻi fell to 1–3, according to The Maui News.

In MIL Division I girls volleyball, King Kekaulike swept Baldwin 3–0 at Baldwin High School Gym on Thursday, Oct. 8. It was one piece of a big run: after sweeping Kamehameha Maui on Oct. 13, King Kekaulike has won seven straight and stands 8–2 in league play, according to ProspectsHI’s HSVB Hub.
