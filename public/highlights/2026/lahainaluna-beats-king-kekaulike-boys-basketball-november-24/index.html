Lahainaluna boys basketball beats King Kekaulike 64–55 to stay unbeaten at 4–0

Published 2026-11-27

The Lunas won the first three quarters and led by 14 going into the fourth in their first MIL game of the season in Pukalani. King Kekaulike then lost to Punahou on Oʻahu.

Lahainaluna kept its perfect start going on Tuesday night, Nov. 24, beating King Kekaulike 64–55 at King Kekaulike High School in Pukalani. It was the first Maui Interscholastic League game of the season for both teams, and the Lunas are now 4–0 overall and 1–0 in league play, according to MaxPreps.

Three strong quarters, then a Nā Aliʻi push

The Lunas controlled the first three quarters. They led 14–9 after one and 31–23 at halftime, then won the third quarter 18–12 to go up 49–35. King Kekaulike made a run at the end, outscoring Lahainaluna 20–15 in the fourth, but the Lunas held on.

  • By quarter: Lahainaluna 14, 17, 18, 15 (64). King Kekaulike 9, 14, 12, 20 (55).
  • Turnaround: King Kekaulike won the last meeting between the two teams 40–39 on Feb. 4, per the MaxPreps game recap.
  • Lahainaluna’s start: wins over ʻAiea (66–55), Island School (66–35) and Seabury Hall (54–49) last week, then Tuesday’s league win. The Lunas have scored 250 points and allowed 194 in four games, an average of 62.5 to 48.5, per their MaxPreps schedule.

Missed it? A replay of Tuesday’s game is on the NFHS Network, King Kekaulike’s streaming partner.

King Kekaulike falls to Punahou

The Nā Aliʻi turned around and played again on Wednesday, Nov. 25, on Oʻahu, falling 64–32 to Punahou in a preseason game, according to the Star-Advertiser scoreboard. King Kekaulike beat ʻAiea 68–56 at home on Nov. 21.
