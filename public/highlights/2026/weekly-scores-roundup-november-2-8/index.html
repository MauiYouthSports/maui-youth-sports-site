Weekly scores roundup, Nov. 2–8: King Kekaulike wins at Kamehameha Maui

Published 2026-11-08

King Kekaulike beat Kamehameha Maui 26–13, three Maui cheer teams scored at the HHSAA championships, and Maui bowling and air riflery teams finished their state meets.

Maui teams were at state meets in cheer, bowling and air riflery, and the MIL football regular season wrapped up. Here’s every final we have confirmed from Monday, Nov. 2, through Sunday, Nov. 8.

Football

King Kekaulike 26, Kamehameha Maui 13 (Friday, Nov. 6, at Kanaʻiaupuni Stadium). Nā Aliʻi won at Kamehameha Maui’s homecoming. King Kekaulike is 6–3 and Kamehameha Maui is 7–2, according to KHON2 and Kamehameha Maui athletics. It was King Kekaulike’s first win in three meetings with Kamehameha Maui this fall. Read our story.

Baldwin and Lahainaluna played Saturday, Nov. 7, at Sue D. Cooley Stadium. We’re still confirming that final and will add it once it checks out. The rest of the slate is in our MIL Football Schedule 2026 guide.

Cheer

Three Maui teams competed in the 12-team All-Girl division at the HHSAA state cheerleading championships on Wednesday, Nov. 4, at the Neal S. Blaisdell Center Arena. Baldwin finished eighth with 35.40 and no deductions. Maui High was 10th with 31.62 (a raw score of 33.37, with 1.75 in deductions). Lahainaluna was 12th with 26.90 and no deductions. Moanalua won with 46.02, Sacred Hearts Academy was second at 44.63, and Punahou was third at 43.23, according to the HHSAA final results. Read the state cheer story.

Bowling

The HHSAA team results for the Nov. 5–6 state bowling championships list three Maui finishes. In girls, Kamehameha Maui was ninth of nine with 4,239 pins. Mililani won with 6,714, Kalani was second with 6,454, and Mid-Pacific was third with 5,605. In boys, Baldwin was eighth of 10 with 5,671 and Kamehameha Maui was ninth with 5,534. Mililani won the boys team title with 7,954, Pearl City was second with 7,036, and Moanalua was third with 6,969.

Air riflery

At the HHSAA air riflery championships on Tuesday, Nov. 3, Maui High’s boys finished seventh of eight with 2,017, according to the boys team results. Moanalua won with 2,124. In girls, Maui High was sixth of eight with 2,016 and Baldwin was seventh with 1,951, per the girls team results. Punahou won the girls title with 2,142.
