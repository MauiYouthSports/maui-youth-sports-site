Weekly scores roundup, Oct. 19–25: King Kekaulike wins the volleyball title, Baldwin and Kamehameha Maui win in football

Published 2026-10-25

Five final scores from the week: King Kekaulike won the MIL Division I girls volleyball crown, Baldwin put up a season-high 48 points against Maui High, and Kamehameha Maui shut out Kūlanihākoʻi 38–0 on Senior Night.

It was a big week for Maui’s high school teams, with a volleyball champion crowned on Tuesday and two football wins to close out the weekend. Here’s every final score we confirmed from Monday, Oct. 19, through Sunday, Oct. 25.

Football

Baldwin 48, Maui High 31 (Friday, Oct. 23, at King Kekaulike). The Bears’ 48 points were their most of the season, according to the MaxPreps recap, and the win stretched their streak to seven straight. Baldwin has now beaten the Sabers three times this fall. Read our full story.

Kamehameha Maui 38, Kūlanihākoʻi 0 (Saturday, Oct. 24, at Kanaʻiaupuni Stadium). The Warriors celebrated Senior Night with their third win of the season over the Manta Rays, according to Kamehameha Maui athletics. All three were shutouts: 63–0 in the season opener, 59–0 on Oct. 2 and now 38–0, a combined 160–0. Kamehameha Maui had already clinched a spot in the HHSAA Division II state tournament with its Oct. 17 win at King Kekaulike, The Maui News reported.

Up next: Maui High visits Lahainaluna on Friday, Oct. 30, at 7 p.m., and Kūlanihākoʻi plays at King Kekaulike on Saturday, Oct. 31, at 6 p.m., per the MIL schedule. Find every remaining game in our MIL Football Schedule 2026 guide.

Girls volleyball

King Kekaulike 3, Lahainaluna 0 and Kamehameha Maui 3, Maui High 0 (Monday, Oct. 19, MIL Division I semifinals at Kamehameha Maui). The Nā Aliʻi’s sweep was their sixth win in a row, per MaxPreps. The Warriors won their semifinal 25–19, 25–21, 25–19, according to Kamehameha Maui athletics.

King Kekaulike 3, Kamehameha Maui 1 (Tuesday, Oct. 20, MIL Division I championship at Kamehameha Maui). The Warriors took the first set 25–20, then King Kekaulike won the next three, including 25–21 in both the second and fourth sets, based on Kamehameha Maui athletics set updates and MaxPreps. Read the championship story or catch up on the whole bracket.

The MIL Division II girls volleyball playoffs were played Oct. 21–23 at Maui Prep, according to the MIL schedule. We’re still confirming those results and will add them once they check out.
