Maui weekend preview, Nov. 20–22: Kamehameha Maui hosts unbeaten Kalaheo in the state semifinals

Published 2026-11-19

The two-time defending Division II champion Warriors host 11–0 Kalaheo Friday night in Pukalani, while Maui County basketball and soccer teams open their preseason on Maui, Molokaʻi and Oʻahu.

It’s semifinal weekend in state football, and this time the big game is right here at home. Kamehameha Maui hosts Friday night with a trip to the state final on the line. Winter sports are getting going too, with MIL basketball and soccer teams playing preseason games on three islands. All times are Hawaiʻi time. Schedules can change, so check with the school or organizer before you head out.

Friday, Nov. 20

  • HHSAA Division II semifinal: No. 1 Kamehameha Maui vs. No. 4 Kalaheo, 6 p.m. Kickoff is at Kanaʻiaupuni Stadium on the KS Maui campus in Pukalani, according to the HHSAA Division II bracket. The Warriors have won the last two Division II state titles and are 7–2 this season. Kalaheo, the OIA champion, is 11–0, according to the Star-Advertiser.
  • Kalaheo’s first-round win: The Mustangs beat Pac-Five 61–21 on Saturday. The official HHSAA game stats show Kalaheo running for 259 yards and seven touchdowns, and its defense picking off five passes, one of them returned 33 yards for a score.
  • Warriors to watch: The Star-Advertiser reports that quarterback Ryder De Silva has thrown 12 touchdown passes with just one interception, and Xander Pagan leads the run game with 697 yards and 10 touchdowns. It also notes this is Kalaheo’s first state-tournament road trip since 2010.
  • Expect a defensive game: MaxPreps has Kamehameha Maui allowing 11.0 points per game and Kalaheo allowing 13.6.
  • Going to the game: State tournament tickets are digital and sold on GoFan, the HHSAA says.
  • What’s next: The winner plays in the Division II state final on Saturday, Nov. 28, at 4 p.m. at John Kauinana Stadium at Mililani High School. The opponent will be the winner of Saturday’s other semifinal, Kaiser at No. 2 Waimea, 2:30 p.m. at Hanapēpē Stadium on Kauaʻi. For more on the Warriors’ season, read our Nov. 13 story.
  • Molokaʻi hosts Kīhei Charter in boys soccer and basketball. Soccer is at 3 p.m. at Molokaʻi High School in Hoʻolehua, and basketball follows at 5 p.m. at Kaunakakai County Gym, according to Molokaʻi’s soccer and basketball event listings.
  • King Kekaulike girls basketball on Oʻahu. Nā Aliʻi play in Nānākuli’s preseason Jesse Baugh Girls Basketball Tournament (event listing), facing Waiʻanae at 4 p.m., according to their MaxPreps schedule.
  • Baldwin girls basketball in Honolulu. The Bears play host Sacred Hearts Academy at 3:30 p.m. at the Sacred Hearts Basketball Invitational, according to their MaxPreps schedule.
  • Baldwin girls soccer at the Pearl City Invitational. The Bears, last season’s MIL girls champions according to Maui Now, take on ʻIolani at 5:30 p.m. at Edwin Bino Neves Stadium at Pearl City High School (event listing).

Saturday, Nov. 21

  • King Kekaulike boys basketball hosts ʻAiea, 6 p.m. The game is in the King Kekaulike gym, according to the school’s event listing. It’s one of the few varsity games on Maui this weekend, so it’s a great chance to catch a preseason game close to home.
  • Molokaʻi vs. Kīhei Charter, round two. The teams meet again in boys soccer at 9 a.m. at Molokaʻi High School and in boys basketball at 11 a.m. at Kaunakakai County Gym.
  • King Kekaulike girls basketball: Nā Aliʻi face Campbell at 8 a.m. on day two of the Nānākuli tournament, according to MaxPreps.
  • Baldwin girls soccer: The Bears face Moanalua on day two of the Pearl City Invitational.
  • Youth softball: Kamehameha Maui’s Nā Koa club teams play at the Maui High softball field: 12U vs. Rize at 2:15 p.m. and 14U vs. Kapili Kakou at 4 p.m., according to the KS Maui athletics schedule.

Around the state

Saint Damien, which ended Baldwin’s season 28–21 last week (our story), plays at No. 1 Waipahu in a Division I semifinal on Friday at 7 p.m., per the HHSAA Division I bracket. The Division I final is also Saturday, Nov. 28, at 7:30 p.m. at Mililani. Every result from the MIL season is in our MIL Football Schedule 2026 guide.

Looking ahead

College basketball comes to Maui next week. The Southwest Maui Invitational runs Monday through Wednesday, Nov. 23–25, at the Lahaina Civic Center, and single-game tickets go on sale Friday at the civic center box office, according to The Maui News.
