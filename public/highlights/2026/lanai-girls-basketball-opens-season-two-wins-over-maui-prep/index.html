Lānaʻi girls basketball opens 2026–27 with two home wins over Maui Prep

Published 2026-11-18

The four-time defending MIL Division II champion Pine Lasses beat the Pueo 71–21 on Friday and 70–18 on Saturday at Pedro dela Cruz Gymnasium.

Lānaʻi’s girls varsity basketball team opened its season with a pair of big wins at home, beating Maui Prep 71–21 on Friday night, Nov. 13, and 70–18 on Saturday morning, Nov. 14. Both games were played at Pedro dela Cruz Gymnasium on the Lānaʻi High & Elementary campus, according to the Star-Advertiser scoreboards for Friday and Saturday.

Friday’s game tipped off at 7 p.m., according to the GoFan event page, and Saturday’s rematch was set for 9 a.m., according to MaxPreps.

The weekend by the numbers

  • Record: Lānaʻi is 2–0 to start the season.
  • Combined score: Lānaʻi 141, Maui Prep 39.
  • Margins: 50 points on Friday and 52 points on Saturday.
  • Defense: The Pine Lasses held the Pueo to 21 points or fewer in both games.

Individual scoring and quarter-by-quarter totals for these two games were not available from the sources we checked. If your team has a box score, send it to us and we’ll add the key stats.

A program that keeps building

Lānaʻi brings plenty of recent success into this season. The Pine Lasses have won four straight Maui Interscholastic League Division II championships, and last winter they reached the HHSAA Division II state final four for the first time in school history, finishing fourth, the Hawaiʻi Journalism Initiative reported in February. The same report said the team was losing only three seniors from an 18-player roster.

The two teams also met last season, when Lānaʻi won 58–21 at Maui Prep on Dec. 19, 2025, according to ScoringLive.

Lānaʻi is next scheduled to play on Thursday, Nov. 19, at Sacred Hearts Academy in Honolulu, according to ScoringLive. Early-season schedules can change, so check with the school for the latest times.

Also on the scoreboard Saturday

Kamehameha Maui’s Nā Koa youth softball teams played three games on Nov. 14, according to KS Maui athletics. At the KS Maui softball field, Kapili Kakou beat the 12U team 20–1 and Rize beat the 14U team 12–6. Later that evening at the Maui High softball field, Kapili Kakou beat the 14U team 15–3.
