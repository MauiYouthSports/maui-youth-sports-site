Maui weekend preview, Nov. 27–29: Kamehameha Maui plays for a third straight state title

Published 2026-11-26

The No. 1 Warriors meet Waimea in Saturday’s Division II football final at Mililani, while Maui County basketball teams play Thanksgiving weekend games at home and on Oʻahu and a free skate jam comes to Pukalani.

Happy Thanksgiving from all of us at Maui Youth Sports. It’s a big holiday weekend: Kamehameha Maui plays for a state football title on Saturday, and MIL basketball teams have games on Maui, in Hāna and on Oʻahu. All times are Hawaiʻi time. Schedules can change, so check with the school or organizer before you head out.

Saturday’s big game: the Division II state final

  • No. 1 Kamehameha Maui vs. No. 2 Waimea, Saturday, Nov. 28, 4 p.m. The game is at John Kauinana Stadium at Mililani High School on Oʻahu, according to the HHSAA Division II bracket.
  • Going for three in a row: The Warriors beat Kaiser 37–14 for the 2024 title and Waimea 48–24 in last year’s final. Waimea won the two titles before that, in 2022 and 2023, and its 2023 win was 31–28 over Kamehameha Maui, according to the HHSAA tournament record book.
  • How they got here: Kamehameha Maui beat Kalaheo 29–11 at home in Pukalani, and Waimea beat Kaiser 36–26 on Kauaʻi, according to the HHSAA bracket. Read our semifinal story for the stats, and our full final preview for the matchup and history.
  • How to watch: The HHSAA football tournament page lists Spectrum OC16 (channel 16, or 1016 in HD), the Spectrum News app, which has open access through Nov. 29, and the Spectrum Local News channel on YouTube.
  • Going to the game: State tournament tickets are digital and sold on GoFan.

Friday, Nov. 27

  • Seabury Hall boys basketball hosts Kūlanihākoʻi, 5 p.m. The game is at Seabury Hall in Makawao, according to the school’s event listing.
  • Hāna hosts Molokaʻi to open the season. The boys play at 5:30 p.m. and the girls at 7 p.m. in Hāna, according to Hāna’s boys and girls event listings.
  • King Kekaulike boys basketball on Oʻahu. Nā Aliʻi play in Kahuku’s North Shore Showdown (event listing) and face Kamehameha-Kapālama at 5:30 p.m., according to ScoringLive and their MaxPreps schedule. On Tuesday, Lahainaluna beat King Kekaulike 64–55 in Pukalani to improve to 4–0, MaxPreps reported.
  • Kīhei Charter boys basketball at Waipahu. Kīhei Charter is one of the teams in Waipahu’s Nii Thanksgiving Basketball Invitational, which runs Friday through Sunday with play starting at 9:30 a.m. each day (event listing).

Saturday, Nov. 28

  • Hāna vs. Molokaʻi, round two. The boys play at 9:30 a.m. and the girls at 11 a.m. in Hāna (boys, girls).
  • King Kekaulike boys basketball: Nā Aliʻi face host Kahuku at 2 p.m. on day three of the North Shore Showdown (event listing), according to ScoringLive and MaxPreps.
  • Pukalani Skate Jam, 3–6 p.m. One Love Skate and the County of Maui Department of Parks and Recreation host a free afternoon at Pukalani Skatepark, with check-in at 2:45 p.m., free lessons from 3 to 3:30 p.m., and contests for different skill levels. Helmets are required, and some gear is available to borrow, according to One Love Skate.
  • Division II final, 4 p.m.: Kamehameha Maui vs. Waimea at Mililani (see above).

Sunday, Nov. 29

  • Kīhei Charter boys basketball wraps up the Nii Thanksgiving Invitational at Waipahu High School, with play starting at 9:30 a.m. (event listing).

Around the state

The Division I final follows on the same field Saturday at 7:30 p.m.: No. 1 Waipahu vs. No. 2 Kapaʻa of Kauaʻi, according to the HHSAA Division I bracket. Waipahu beat Saint Damien 42–0 in the semifinals, and Kapaʻa beat Kamehameha-Hawaiʻi 24–13. Saint Damien ended Baldwin’s season in the first round (our story). Every MIL result from this fall is in our MIL Football Schedule 2026 guide.

Signups

Maui Stars Hawaii’s Winter 2027 flag football seasons start in January, and team submissions are due by Monday, Nov. 30, according to the league’s events page. Catch up on how the fall season ended in our fall wrap-up.
