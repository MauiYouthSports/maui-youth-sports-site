Fall youth flag football registration enters final days of regular pricing

Published 2026-09-27 · Historical article

Maui Sports Leagues’ Phase 1 Fall 2026 youth flag football registration remains open, with regular registration scheduled through Sept. 30.

The fall league lists coed divisions from 4U through 14U and girls divisions from 8U through 16U. The season is scheduled for Oct. 17 through Dec. 12, with league-wide breaks on Halloween and Thanksgiving weekend.

Regular registration is listed at $120. Late registration is scheduled from Oct. 1 through Oct. 24 at $140, subject to league availability and policies.
