Fall-break junior golf clinic set for October 7 at The Dunes

Published 2026-10-04

HSJGA lists a junior skills clinic at The Dunes at Maui Lani on Wednesday, Oct. 7, from 4 to 5:30 p.m.

The Hawaiʻi State Junior Golf Association announced fall-break instruction for junior golfers at The Dunes at Maui Lani on Oct. 7, from 4 to 5:30 p.m.

The clinic covers swing technique and golf fundamentals with PGA and LPGA instructors. The published fee is $49, including a hat and refreshments. Check the organizer’s registration link for available places.
