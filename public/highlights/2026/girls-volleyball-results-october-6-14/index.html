Girls volleyball: King Kekaulike sweeps two, Seabury Hall stays perfect

Published 2026-10-15

King Kekaulike swept Lahainaluna and Kamehameha Maui, Baldwin and Maui High each won a five-set match, and Seabury Hall improved to 10–0 in MIL Division II.

King Kekaulike had a big stretch in MIL Division I girls volleyball. It beat Lahainaluna 3–0 (25–20, 25–20, 25–21) at home on Tuesday, Oct. 6, then swept Kamehameha Maui 3–0 (25–22, 25–17, 27–25) on Tuesday, Oct. 13.

Also on Oct. 6, Kamehameha Maui won 3–1 at Baldwin (27–25, 22–25, 26–24, 25–15). On Thursday, Oct. 8, Maui High dropped the first two sets to Lahainaluna, then came back to win 3–2 (17–25, 22–25, 25–15, 25–16, 15–10). Maui High went five again on Oct. 13, and this time Baldwin won it at home, 3–2 (22–25, 25–15, 11–25, 25–16, 15–8).

In Division II, Seabury Hall swept Maui Prep 3–0 (25–16, 25–16, 25–19) at home on Wednesday, Oct. 14. That’s 10 straight wins and a 10–0 league record for the Spartans, who host Kūlanihākoʻi on Friday, Oct. 16, at 6 p.m.
