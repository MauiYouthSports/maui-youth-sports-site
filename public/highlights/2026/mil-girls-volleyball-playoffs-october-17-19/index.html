MIL girls volleyball playoffs: Kamehameha Maui and King Kekaulike reach the Division I final

Published 2026-10-21

Lahainaluna beat Baldwin in Saturday’s play-in. King Kekaulike beat Lahainaluna 3–0 on Oct. 19, then won the MIL Division I championship 3–1 over Kamehameha Maui on Oct. 20 at Kamehameha Maui.

The MIL Division I girls volleyball playoffs got going on Saturday, Oct. 17, and by Monday night the field was down to two: Kamehameha Maui and King Kekaulike.

Lunas win the play-in at home

Lahainaluna opened the playoffs with a 3–1 win over Baldwin at Lahainaluna High School Gym (25–20, 25–22, 15–25, 25–20). Baldwin took the third set and kept the other three close, according to the MaxPreps recap.

Two sweeps in the semifinals

Both semifinals were played Monday, Oct. 19, at Kamehameha Maui’s Kaʻulaheanuiokamoku Gymnasium. King Kekaulike moved on with a 3–0 win over Lahainaluna, and MaxPreps reports it was the Nā Aliʻi’s sixth straight win.

In the night match, Kamehameha Maui swept Maui High 3–0 (25–19, 25–21, 25–19), according to the Kamehameha Maui athletics schedule.

King Kekaulike wins the Division I title

King Kekaulike won the 2026 MIL Division I girls volleyball championship, beating Kamehameha Maui 3–1 on Tuesday, Oct. 20, at Kamehameha Maui, according to MaxPreps. Read the championship story.
