King Kekaulike beats Kamehameha Maui 26–13 in MIL football

Published 2026-11-07

Nā Aliʻi win 26–13 at Kamehameha Maui’s homecoming, taking the season series finale after two earlier losses.

King Kekaulike beat Kamehameha Maui 26–13 in Maui Interscholastic League Division I football on Friday night, Nov. 6, at Kanaʻiaupuni Stadium, according to KHON2 and Kamehameha Maui athletics. The game was Kamehameha Maui’s homecoming.

First win in the season series

It was the third King Kekaulike–Kamehameha Maui meeting of the 2026 season, and Nā Aliʻi’s first win in the series this fall. Kamehameha Maui won the first two: 31–7 on Sept. 18 and 27–14 on Oct. 17. The Oct. 17 win also secured Kamehameha Maui an HHSAA Division II state tournament berth, according to The Maui News.

What the result means

After Friday’s game, King Kekaulike is 6–3 and Kamehameha Maui is 7–2, according to KHON2. Baldwin had already locked up the MIL Division I title and a state berth with its Oct. 16 win at Lahainaluna, The Maui News reported. Kamehameha Maui already held its Division II state berth before Friday’s homecoming.

Up next

Baldwin plays Lahainaluna on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 7 p.m. at Sue D. Cooley Stadium, 980 Lahainaluna Rd, Lahaina. See our MIL Football Schedule 2026 guide and check with the schools before you go.
