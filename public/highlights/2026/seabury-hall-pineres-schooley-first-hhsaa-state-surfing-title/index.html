Seabury Hall’s Kahlil Pineres-Schooley is Maui’s first HHSAA state surfing champion

Published 2026-11-20

At the first-ever HHSAA Surfing State Championships last May at Hoʻokipa, he won the boys shortboard final with a 14.27 total. We’re adding the inaugural state results to our records.

Hawaiʻi crowned its first high school state surfing champions on May 1–2, 2026, at Hoʻokipa Beach Park on Maui’s North Shore, and one of them came from right here at home. Seabury Hall’s Kahlil Pineres-Schooley won the boys shortboard title at the inaugural HHSAA Surfing State Championships, making him the first state surfing champion from a Maui school. MYS is adding those first state results to our records now, so Maui families can find them alongside this year’s scores.

The winning final

  • Champion: Kahlil Pineres-Schooley, Seabury Hall, 14.27 (wave scores of 6.6 and 7.67), according to the official LiveHeats results.
  • Runner-up: Hayden Flores, Kapaʻa, 13.20.
  • Also: Pineres-Schooley tied for 10th in boys longboard.

He took second in each of his earlier heats, from the opening round through the semifinals, then posted the best single wave of the final, The Maui News reported. He was a sophomore at the time.

“We’ve all grown up at this wave. This is our home break,” Pineres-Schooley told The Maui News after the final. MIL surfing co-coordinator Kim Ball told the paper, “He was the first state champion ever in the state of Hawaii, so that’s even better.”

A Maui-heavy final

Four of the six boys in the shortboard final surfed for Maui Interscholastic League schools: King Kekaulike’s Chase Burnes was third (12.63), Lahainaluna’s Matias Banto fourth (10.00) and Maui Prep’s Zolten Poulsen sixth (8.24).

Maui surfers at state

The two-day meet, hosted by the MIL, drew 174 surfers and bodyboarders from all five Hawaiʻi high school leagues, with 36 boys teams and 27 girls teams, according to The Maui News. Here’s how Maui schools did, from the LiveHeats event results:

Top individual finishes

  • Sloane Jucker, Maui Prep: 2nd, girls shortboard (10.17)
  • Zolten Poulsen, Maui Prep: 3rd, boys longboard (12.66)
  • Chase Burnes, King Kekaulike: 3rd, boys shortboard (12.63)
  • Matias Banto, Lahainaluna: 4th, boys shortboard (10.00)
  • Bode Davis, Kūlanihākoʻi: 4th, boys longboard (11.70)
  • Hiʻipoi Starbuck, Kamehameha Maui: 4th, girls bodyboard (5.73)
  • Ocean Lipstein, Lahainaluna: 5th, girls shortboard (7.47)
  • Makoa Kapuniai, King Kekaulike: 6th, boys bodyboard (4.53)

Boys team standings

  • State champion: Kahuku, 3,150 points; runner-up: Kapaʻa, 2,935
  • Maui Prep: 3rd of 36, 2,931
  • King Kekaulike: 5th, 2,181
  • Seabury Hall: 10th, 1,488
  • Lahainaluna: 13th, 1,060
  • Also scoring: Kūlanihākoʻi 670, Haleakalā Waldorf 555, Maui Christian Academy 500, Baldwin 475

Girls team standings

  • State champion: Waialua, 4,120 points; runner-up: Kapolei, 3,150
  • Lahainaluna: 5th of 27, 2,770
  • Maui Prep: 7th, 2,100
  • Maui Christian Academy: 9th, 1,828
  • King Kekaulike: 13th, 1,325
  • Also scoring: Kamehameha Maui 670, Kīhei Charter 450

Maui Prep’s third-place boys finish was the best team result for an MIL school. More on the event is on the HHSAA surfing tournament page.
