MIL Football Schedule 2026: Remaining Games, Results and State Tournament Dates

Published 2026-10-21

Every remaining Maui Interscholastic League football game, the results so far, and the dates for the HHSAA state tournament in one place.

Last updated Oct. 23, 2026

This MIL Football Schedule 2026 guide covers the rest of the Maui Interscholastic League regular season, the results we’ve confirmed so far, and what comes next at the state tournament. Dates, sites and kickoff times come from the official MIL Division I football schedule, and we’ll keep this page updated as games are played.

How MIL football is set up in 2026

The MIL plays one 11-player varsity schedule with six schools: Baldwin, Kamehameha Maui, King Kekaulike, Kūlanihākoʻi, Lahainaluna and Maui High. The league posts it as Division I football. Hāna, Lānaʻi and Molokaʻi play a separate eight-player schedule. The MIL doesn’t post a separate Division II football schedule, but the HHSAA gives the league one Division I spot and one Division II spot at the state tournament.

Who has clinched

Baldwin locked up the MIL Division I title with its 13–3 win at Lahainaluna on Oct. 16, which also earned the Bears a state tournament berth, according to The Maui News. It’s the Bears’ second league title in a row.

In eight-player football, The Maui News reports that Molokaʻi beat Hāna 24–19 on Oct. 17 to win the MIL championship. The eight-player regular season ended Oct. 10, according to the MIL eight-player schedule. Read our story on Molokaʻi’s title.
