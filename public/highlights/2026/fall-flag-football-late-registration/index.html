Fall youth flag football enters late registration ahead of October 17 kickoff

Published 2026-10-04

Maui Sports Leagues lists late registration through Oct. 24 at $140, with the fall season scheduled to begin Oct. 17.

Regular pricing ended Sept. 30. Maui Sports Leagues’ published late-registration window runs Oct. 1–24 at $140.

The season is scheduled Oct. 17–Dec. 12, with coed 4U–14U and girls 8U–16U divisions. Families must obtain a team coach’s approval before registering; the league states that payments are nonrefundable. Confirm space and team placement directly with the league.
