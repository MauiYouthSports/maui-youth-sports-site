Weekly scores roundup, Nov. 16–22: Kamehameha Maui reaches the state final as Maui basketball and soccer seasons begin

Published 2026-11-22

Kamehameha Maui beat Kalaheo 29–11 to set up a Division II title rematch with Waimea, Lahainaluna boys basketball moved to 3–0 with a 54–49 win at Seabury Hall, and Baldwin girls soccer opened with a tie and a shutout win.

Football is down to one Maui team, and the winter seasons are underway. Here’s every final we’ve checked from Monday, Nov. 16, through Sunday, Nov. 22.

Football

Kamehameha Maui 29, Kalaheo 11 (Friday, Nov. 20, at Kanaʻiaupuni Stadium in Pukalani). The top-seeded Warriors trailed 3–0 after the first quarter, then scored the next 29 points to hand Kalaheo its first loss of the season in the HHSAA Division II semifinals, according to the HHSAA bracket. Kamehameha Maui is 8–2, per The Maui News. Key numbers from the official HHSAA stats:

  • Xander Pagan ran 21 times for 95 yards and a touchdown, and the Warriors totaled 206 rushing yards.
  • Kaikea Hueu scored twice, on a 21-yard fumble return and a 6-yard run.
  • The defense intercepted three passes (Kamakana Casco, Urijah Benz-Ah Yen and Teyvon Kahalewai) and was credited with seven sacks, led by Kapena Lani’s 2.5.
  • Kalaheo standout running back Marquez Mellor was held to 42 yards on 11 carries.

Read the full game story.

Boys basketball

The preseason tipped off with a tournament at the Seabury Hall gym in Makawao, according to MaxPreps recaps.

Seabury Hall 63, Island School 28 (Thursday) and Seabury Hall 70, ʻAiea 46 (Friday). The Spartans opened their home schedule with a 35-point win, then led ʻAiea 56–31 after three quarters on Friday to move to 2–0 (Thursday recap, Friday recap).

Lahainaluna 66, ʻAiea 55 (Thursday) and Lahainaluna 66, Island School 35 (Friday). The Lunas scored 66 points in both games. On Friday they led 45–10 at halftime and moved to 2–0 (Thursday recap, Friday recap).

Lahainaluna 54, Seabury Hall 49 (Saturday, Nov. 21, in Makawao). The Lunas won at Seabury Hall to move to 3–0. It was the Spartans’ first loss, leaving them 2–1, per MaxPreps.

King Kekaulike 68, ʻAiea 56 (Saturday, Nov. 21, in Pukalani). Na Aliʻi won every quarter and evened their record at 1–1, per MaxPreps.

No player stats were posted for these games.

Girls basketball

Lānaʻi 58, Island School 50 (Thursday, Nov. 19, at the Sacred Hearts Invitational in Honolulu), per the Star-Advertiser scoreboard. The Pine Lasses opened the week before with two home wins over Maui Prep. Read that story.

Girls soccer

Baldwin opened its season at the Pearl City Invitational. Baldwin 2, ʻIolani 2 on Thursday was the first draw of the season for the Raiders, per MaxPreps. On Saturday, the Bears blanked Moanalua, Baldwin 2, Moanalua 0 (MaxPreps).

What’s next

Football: Kamehameha Maui plays No. 2 Waimea for the HHSAA Division II state title on Saturday, Nov. 28, at 4 p.m. at John Kauinana Stadium at Mililani High School. Waimea beat Kaiser 36–26 in the other semifinal on Saturday, Nov. 21, at Hanapēpē Stadium on Kauaʻi, according to the HHSAA bracket. It’s a rematch of last year’s final, which the Warriors won 48–24 for their second straight title. The Maui News notes that Waimea beat Kamehameha Maui 31–28 for the 2023 title, so the two schools have won the last four Division II championships between them. The Division I final, No. 1 Waipahu vs. No. 2 Kapaʻa, follows at 7:30 p.m., per the Division I bracket. The HHSAA says tickets go on sale on GoFan the Sunday night before each game. Every result and the finals are in our MIL Football Schedule 2026 guide.

Girls soccer: Baldwin hosts Maui on Sunday, Nov. 22, at 7 p.m., the Bears’ first MIL game, per MaxPreps.

Boys basketball: King Kekaulike hosts Lahainaluna on Tuesday, Nov. 24, in Na Aliʻi’s first MIL game, per MaxPreps. Check with the schools for the tip-off time.

In case you missed it

Baldwin’s football season ended with a 28–21 loss to Saint Damien in the state first round on Nov. 14. Read the story.
