Weekend roundup: Kamehameha Maui tops King Kekaulike, Baldwin wins at Lahainaluna

Published 2026-10-18

Kamehameha Maui beat King Kekaulike 27–14, Baldwin won at Lahainaluna 13–3, and Seabury Hall and Hāna won Friday night in girls volleyball.

Kamehameha Maui won the big Saturday night game, beating King Kekaulike 27–14 on the Nā Aliʻi’s home field on Oct. 17. KHON2 had called it a big MIL showdown. It was the Warriors’ second win over King Kekaulike this season, after a 31–7 win on Sept. 18.

On Friday, Oct. 16, Baldwin won a low-scoring game at Lahainaluna, 13–3. MaxPreps reports it was the Bears’ sixth straight win and their second over the Lunas this season, after a 27–19 win on Sept. 18. Next up, according to MaxPreps, Baldwin visits Maui High this Friday night, and Lahainaluna hosts Maui High on Oct. 30.

In MIL Division II girls volleyball on Friday night, Seabury Hall swept Kūlanihākoʻi 3–0 (25–13, 25–16, 25–8) at home in Makawao. Hāna beat Lānaʻi 3–1 (25–19, 25–17, 24–26, 25–15) at Hāna School Gym, with Lānaʻi taking a tight third set 26–24, according to ProspectsHI’s HSVB Hub.

Hāna and Lānaʻi met again on Saturday morning in Hāna. We’re still confirming that score and will add it once it checks out.
