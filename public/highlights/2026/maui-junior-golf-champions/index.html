Maui golfers Austin and Lee win divisions at Maui Junior Championship

Published 2026-09-01 · Historical article

Two Maui junior golfers finished atop their divisions at the Maui Junior Championship at Kāʻanapali Royal Golf Course. Artie Austin of Wailuku won the 9-Hole Flight, while Lakota Lee of Kīhei won the Boys 15–18 division.

The Hawaiʻi State Junior Golf Association | First Tee – Hawaiʻi event was played Aug. 29–30 and drew 50 junior golfers from around the state. The 36-hole championship also awarded points toward the association’s Player of the Year standings and December Tour Championship.

For Maui families, the results put two local players at the top of their respective divisions in a statewide junior field on a Maui course.
