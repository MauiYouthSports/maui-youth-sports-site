Maui weekend preview, Nov. 6–8: MIL football finales and club volleyball tryouts

Published 2026-11-06

The last two MIL Division I football games of the regular season land Friday and Saturday, Aloha Volleyball Association holds high school girls club tryouts Sunday, and we’re catching up on Maui County’s MIL and state cross country results.

It’s the last weekend of the MIL football regular season, plus a club volleyball tryout day on Sunday. Here’s what to watch Nov. 6–8, with a catch-up on cross country results that weren’t on our highlights yet. All times are Hawaiʻi time, and it’s always smart to check with the schools before you go.

Catching up: MIL and state cross country

MIL championships, Oct. 24 at Baldwin High School. Seabury Hall won the boys team title with 20 points, ahead of Maui High (77) and King Kekaulike (87), according to The Maui News. Silas Miller-Yoder of Seabury Hall won the boys 5K in 17:09 and led a Seabury sweep of the top four (Brady Punu 17:24, Remi Kuriger 17:57, Brendan Punu 17:58).

Kamehameha Maui won the girls team title with 36 points, ahead of Maui High (63), Kūlanihākoʻi (69) and King Kekaulike (77). Keilana Maldonado of Kamehameha Maui won the girls race in 20:19, ahead of Brooke Hoida of Kīhei Charter (20:32) and Ariceli Gonzales of King Kekaulike (20:55). The Maui News also reports that Kūlanihākoʻi celebrated its first MIL Division II girls cross country title.

HHSAA state meet, Oct. 31 at Hawaiʻi Preparatory Academy in Waimea. Per the HHSAA combined team results:

  • Boys Division II: Seabury Hall finished second with 44 points behind champion Hawaiʻi Prep (25). Silas Miller-Yoder was fifth overall (second in DII) in 17:40, and Brady Punu was sixth overall in 17:44.
  • Boys Division I: King Kekaulike placed 10th of 16 with 239 points (Makoa Kapuniai 19:25). Maui High placed 12th with 314 (Ian Madriaga 20:00).
  • Girls Division I: Kamehameha Maui placed ninth of 16 with 254 points; Keilana Maldonado ran 22:12. Maui High placed 13th with 314 (Jozea Sanchez-Cabrera 25:09).

A student-athlete profile on Silas Miller-Yoder is with Rubio as permission pending for Wednesday go-live.

Friday, Nov. 6

  • MIL football: King Kekaulike at Kamehameha Maui, 7 p.m. On the MIL Division I football schedule and the MIL calendar. King Kekaulike is 5–3 after a 48–7 win over Kūlanihākoʻi on Oct. 31, according to MaxPreps and the Star-Advertiser scoreboard. Kamehameha Maui already has an HHSAA Division II state berth, according to The Maui News. Full remaining slate: our MIL Football Schedule 2026 guide.

Saturday, Nov. 7

  • MIL football: Baldwin vs. Lahainaluna, 7 p.m. The game is at Sue D. Cooley Stadium, 980 Lahainaluna Rd, Lahaina. Baldwin locked up the MIL Division I title and a state berth with its Oct. 16 win at Lahainaluna, according to The Maui News. Lahainaluna is 3–5 after edging Maui High 13–10 on Oct. 30, per MaxPreps.

Sunday, Nov. 8

  • Aloha Volleyball Association high school girls club tryouts, 10:45 a.m.–12:15 p.m., South Maui Gym in Kīhei. Still listed on AVA’s indoor club page and sign-up form. Bring water, knee pads, court shoes and the signed waiver and medical release. Full tryout details.

What’s next in football

After this weekend, the HHSAA football tournament opens with Open Division semifinals on Nov. 13 and Division I / Division II first-round games on Nov. 14, according to the HHSAA football tournament page. Championships for DI and DII are set for Nov. 28 at Mililani High School.

We’ll have the weekend’s scores in Sunday’s weekly roundup. Have a score or photo to share? Send it to us.
