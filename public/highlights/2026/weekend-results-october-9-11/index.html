Weekend roundup: King Kekaulike rolls, Lahainaluna edges Maui; Seabury Hall and Hāna sweep volleyball weekends

Published 2026-10-14

King Kekaulike beat Kūlanihākoʻi 53–0, Lahainaluna held off Maui High 15–12, and Seabury Hall and Hāna each won twice in girls volleyball.

King Kekaulike shut out Kūlanihākoʻi 53–0 on Friday, Oct. 9, its fourth straight home win. On Saturday, Oct. 10, Lahainaluna held off Maui High 15–12 at home in its first conference game.

In girls volleyball on Friday, Oct. 9, Seabury Hall beat Lānaʻi 3–0, Hāna beat Kīhei Charter 3–0 and Kūlanihākoʻi beat Haleakalā Waldorf 3–0. On Saturday, Oct. 10, Seabury Hall beat Lānaʻi again, 3–1, extending its winning streak to 11. Hāna also won again at Kīhei Charter, 3–0 (25–15, 25–8, 25–10).

No Sunday, Oct. 11, results were confirmed in the sources reviewed. Missing results do not mean no games were played.
