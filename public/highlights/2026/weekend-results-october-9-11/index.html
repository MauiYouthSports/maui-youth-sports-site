Weekend roundup: King Kekaulike rolls, Lahainaluna edges Maui; Hāna sweeps volleyball

Published 2026-10-14

In football, King Kekaulike beat Kūlanihākoʻi 53–0 and Lahainaluna held off Maui High 15–12; in girls volleyball, Hāna won twice and Seabury Hall beat Lānaʻi 3–1.

In football, King Kekaulike shut out Kūlanihākoʻi 53–0 on Friday, Oct. 9, its fourth straight home win. On Saturday, Oct. 10, Lahainaluna held off Maui High 15–12 at home in its first conference game.

In girls volleyball on Friday, Oct. 9, Hāna beat Kīhei Charter 3–0 and Kūlanihākoʻi beat Haleakalā Waldorf 3–0. On Saturday, Oct. 10, Seabury Hall beat Lānaʻi 3–1, and Hāna beat Kīhei Charter 3–0 (25–15, 25–8, 25–10).
