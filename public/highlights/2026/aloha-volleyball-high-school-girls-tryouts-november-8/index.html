Aloha Volleyball Association high school girls club tryouts set for Nov. 8 in Kīhei

Published 2026-10-26

Aloha Volleyball Association is holding tryouts for its 2026–27 high school girls indoor club team on Sunday, Nov. 8, at South Maui Gym in Kīhei.

Got a high school volleyball player who wants more time on the court after the school season? Aloha Volleyball Association (AVA) has posted tryouts for its 2026–27 high school girls indoor club, and sign-ups are open now.

Tryout details

  • When: Sunday, Nov. 8, 10:45 a.m. to 12:15 p.m.
  • Where: South Maui Gym, 1501 Liloa Dr., Kīhei
  • Who: High school girls. The sign-up form includes AVA’s player age definition, so check it before you register.
  • Sign up: on AVA’s tryout sign-up form.
  • Bring: water, knee pads and court shoes, plus the signed AVA waiver and medical release form.

Both forms are on AVA’s Indoor Club page: the AVA waiver (PDF) and the medical release form (PDF). Filling them out ahead of time saves a scramble at the gym door.

What club volleyball costs

AVA is upfront that club play has three costs: club dues, a uniform and travel. Its Indoor Club page puts the uniform at about $150 to $225. Travel depends on how many players go. AVA estimates $800 to $1,200 per player for an outer-island trip and $1,500 to $2,000 for a Mainland trip, not counting airfare, which families cover. Dues vary by skill level, travel and season length, so ask the club for the current amount.

About AVA

AVA is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit that has been promoting volleyball in Maui County since 2004, through training, competitions and work on local courts, including nets and equipment for county sand courts, according to its About page. It also runs youth volleyball for ages 7 to 10.

Questions? AVA asks families to email info@alohavolleyball.com.
