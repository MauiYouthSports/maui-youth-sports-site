Weekend roundup: KS Maui football wins; Seabury Hall and Molokaʻi take volleyball matches

Published 2026-10-04

Friday’s published finals include KS Maui’s 59–0 football win and volleyball victories for Seabury Hall and Molokaʻi.

Kamehameha Maui shut out Kulanihakoʻi 59–0 on Friday, Oct. 2. In girls volleyball, Seabury Hall defeated Maui Prep 3–0 and Molokaʻi beat Hāna 3–1.

These are Friday results. The Saturday Molokaʻi–Hāna rematch, Maui–Baldwin football, Hāna–Lānaʻi football, JV jamboree and MIL cross-country placings remain unconfirmed in the sources reviewed. Missing results do not mean no games were played.
