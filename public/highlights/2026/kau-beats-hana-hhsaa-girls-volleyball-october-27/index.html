Kaʻū beats Hāna 3–1 in HHSAA Division II first round

Published 2026-10-27

Hāna took the opening set at Kalani, then Kaʻū won the next three to advance to Wednesday’s quarterfinals.

Kaʻū beat Hāna 3–1 in the HHSAA Division II girls volleyball first round on Tuesday, Oct. 27, at Kalani High School, according to the HHSAA Division II bracket.

Set by set

Hāna won the first set 25–21. Kaʻū answered 25–21 in the second, edged a long third set 27–25, and closed it out 25–8 in the fourth, according to the HHSAA match stats.

Key stats

For Kaʻū, Zia Wroblewski had 17 kills, 2 aces and 11 digs, and Harlem Reyes-Espejo finished with 11 kills and 16 digs. Setter Kiar Ortega-Oliveira recorded 29 assists and 3 aces, per the HHSAA box score.

For Hāna, Java Cantero led with 11 kills, 3 aces and 15 digs. Mahea Sinenci had 10 kills and 3 aces, Destiny Perry had 22 assists, and Jaelyn Sinenci dug up 19 balls.

What the bracket shows next

Kaʻū moves into Wednesday’s quarterfinals against No. 3 Waipahu at Kalani (7 p.m.), according to the HHSAA bracket. Hāna drops into the Division II consolation bracket, where first-round losers continue playing on Wednesday.
