Free Surf-N-Turf youth program heads into final September session

Published 2026-09-27

Mana O Maui’s free Surf-N-Turf afterschool program is scheduled to hold the final session of its September series on Wednesday, Sept. 30.

The program serves Maui youth ages 11 to 17 and combines surfing or hiking depending on conditions. The September series was scheduled for four Wednesdays, with transportation offered from Central Maui pickup points.

The program is designed to get young people outdoors after school while providing structured recreation and access to Maui’s natural environment.
