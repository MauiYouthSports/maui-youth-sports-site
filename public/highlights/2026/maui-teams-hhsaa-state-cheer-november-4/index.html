Baldwin, Maui High and Lahainaluna represent Maui at state cheer

Published 2026-11-07

Baldwin finished eighth, Maui High 10th and Lahainaluna 12th in the 12-team All-Girl division at the Nov. 4 HHSAA state cheerleading championships.

Three Maui teams took their place on the state cheerleading floor Wednesday, Nov. 4, at Honolulu’s Neal S. Blaisdell Center Arena. Baldwin led the island’s finishers at the HHSAA championships, placing eighth in the All-Girl division.

Maui’s state results

The official HHSAA final results list 12 All-Girl teams. Here is how Maui’s three representatives finished:

  • Baldwin: eighth, 35.40. The Bears had no deductions.
  • Maui High: 10th, 31.62. Maui High’s raw score was 33.37, with 1.75 in deductions.
  • Lahainaluna: 12th, 26.90. Lahainaluna had no deductions.

Moanalua won the All-Girl championship with 46.02. Sacred Hearts Academy finished second at 44.63, followed by Punahou at 43.23.

The state meet followed Maui’s league championship, where Baldwin, Maui High and Lahainaluna earned the three qualifying spots. Read our MIL cheerleading championship story for that lead-up.

Mahalo to all three teams for representing Maui on the state stage.
