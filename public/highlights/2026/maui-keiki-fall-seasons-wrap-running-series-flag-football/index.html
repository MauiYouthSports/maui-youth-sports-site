Maui keiki wrap fall seasons in running and flag football: results and what’s next

Published 2026-11-24

The Healthy Kids Running Series closed its fall season in Kahului, and Maui Stars Hawaii crowned three youth flag football champions. Winter flag football team submissions are due Nov. 30.

While the high school spotlight is on state football and the first winter games, two of Maui’s biggest keiki programs just finished their fall seasons. Here’s how they wrapped up and where young athletes can play next.

Healthy Kids Running Series: a five-week finish in Kahului

The Healthy Kids Running Series Maui held its fall finale on Sunday, Nov. 15, on the back field at Pōmaikaʻi Elementary School in Kahului, according to The Maui News. The series ran five straight Sundays starting Oct. 18, with races for keiki ages 2 to 14, from 50- and 75-yard dashes for the youngest runners up to a full mile for older kids (Maui News preview).

Runners earned points for each race, and season points decided the division titles. The Maui News published final standings for eight divisions, split into boys and girls for kindergarten–first grade, second–third, fourth–fifth and sixth–eighth grade.

  • Near-perfect seasons: Six runners finished with a perfect 50 points to win their divisions, and two more won titles with 49, per The Maui News.
  • Fastest finale miles: The fourth- and fifth-grade boys winner ran the mile in 6:06, and the sixth- through eighth-grade boys winner finished in 6:27. On the girls side, the winning miles were 7:21 (fourth–fifth grade) and 7:22 (sixth–eighth grade).
  • Shorter races: The second- and third-grade half-mile winners ran 3:09 (girls) and 3:12 (boys). In the kindergarten–first grade quarter-mile, the winners clocked 1:24 (boys) and 1:36 (girls).
  • Parents ran too: Thirty parents and guardians lined up for a 1-mile race, and the winner finished in 5:50.
  • Everyone went home with something: Every runner received a finisher medal, and the top three in each division earned trophies. A runner was also honored with the season’s Healthy Kid Award for attitude, sportsmanship and commitment to improvement.

The finale doubled as a keiki celebration, with activities from 808 Gymnastics, Maui Taekwondo and Shaka Slides, plus shave ice from Waikomo. The full division results are posted in The Maui News.

The program keeps growing. Organizers welcomed 227 runners in the spring season, Maui Now reported. The series runs in the spring and fall, so watch the Healthy Kids Running Series Maui page for the next season’s dates.

Maui Stars Hawaii flag football crowns three champions

Maui Stars Hawaii, a community nonprofit flag football league, has posted its final fall 2026 youth standings. The champions, according to the league’s Programs & Events page:

  • 7U: Viperz 7U, unbeaten at 7–0, outscoring opponents 269–53.
  • 9U: Viperz 9U, also 7–0, with 305 points scored and 51 allowed.
  • 11U: Outlaws 11U (7–3), who scored a division-best 387 points. Swagger Squad 11U finished with the best record at 8–2.

Hui Kulamalu fielded teams in all three divisions, including girls teams at 7U and 9U.

What’s next for Maui keiki

  • Winter flag football: Maui Stars Hawaii’s Winter 2027 youth and adult seasons kick off in January. Team submissions are due by Monday, Nov. 30, through the Maui Stars Hawaii website.
  • Skate Jam in Lahaina: Maui County Parks and Recreation and One Love Skate host a free Skate Jam on Saturday, Dec. 5, from 3 to 6 p.m. at Lahaina Skate Park. It’s open to all ages and skill levels, with timed runs and best-trick contests, food, games, prizes and music, according to The Maui News. Registration opens at 2:45 p.m., per Maui Now. Helmets are required, and a limited amount of skateboarding gear will be available to borrow. More info: Maui County Parks and Recreation.
  • Running: Watch for the spring dates from the Healthy Kids Running Series Maui.
