Free UH junior golf clinic coming to Kāʻanapali October 27

Published 2026-10-04

Kāʻanapali Golf Courses lists a complimentary clinic with the UH men’s golf team on Tuesday, Oct. 27, from 3 to 4 p.m.

Maui keiki can learn with the University of Hawaiʻi men’s golf team at a complimentary clinic listed for Oct. 27, from 3 to 4 p.m.

The clinic is part of the Kāʻanapali Classic activities. Use the golf course’s official clinic registration link to sign up and get arrival details.
