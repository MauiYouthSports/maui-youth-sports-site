Maui Youth Air Riflery

Last checked Nov 2026

On Maui, air riflery is a high school sport. Boys' and girls' teams from schools across Maui and Molokaʻi compete in the Maui Interscholastic League (MIL) every fall, from early September through the MIL Championships in October, with top shooters going on to the HHSAA state championships on Oʻahu. If your teen is interested, the path starts with their school's coach or athletic office.

What air riflery is

Air riflery is a precision target sport. Athletes shoot air rifles at paper targets on a supervised range, and scores come down to focus, breathing, patience and consistency, not size or speed. That's why it can be a great fit for students who haven't found their sport yet.

Safety comes first. Practices and matches are run by trained school coaches under strict range rules set by the schools, the MIL and the HHSAA. Students learn everything on their school's range, under supervision. School ranges aren't open to the public.

Which Maui County schools have teams

These Maui County schools field air riflery teams:

  • Central Maui: Baldwin (Wailuku) and Maui High (Kahului)
  • Upcountry: King Kekaulike and Kamehameha Schools Maui (Pukalani), and Seabury Hall (Makawao)
  • South Maui: Kūlanihākoʻi (Kīhei)
  • East Maui: Hāna (coach to be announced)
  • Molokaʻi: Molokaʻi High
  • Pāʻia: Maui Christian Academy

Lānaʻi High doesn't currently list air riflery. Rosters and programs can change from year to year, so check with your school each season. Other precision sports include archery.

How the MIL season works

Air riflery is a fall sport. The 2026 MIL season mixed in-person matches at host schools with "postal" rounds, where teams shoot on their own range and scores are compared across the league. The 2026 schedule looked like this:

  • Sept. 5: season opener at Maui High
  • Sept. 12, Sept. 26 and Oct. 10: postal rounds
  • Sept. 19: match at Baldwin ROTC
  • Oct. 17: MIL Championships at Kamehameha Schools Maui
  • Nov. 3: HHSAA State Championships at the Neal Blaisdell Exhibition Hall, Honolulu

Expect a similar rhythm next fall, with dates confirmed by the MIL each summer. See the MIL calendar and schedules on MYS.

2026 results

Maui High swept the 2026 MIL titles. Its girls won with 2,032 points and its boys with 1,992. At the HHSAA state championships on Nov. 3, Maui High's girls placed sixth and its boys seventh, and Baldwin's girls placed seventh. Read the full weekly roundup, and follow scores all season.

How to join your school team

  1. Talk to the coach or athletic director at the end of the school year or over the summer. Seasons start early. Seabury Hall's 2026 season began Aug. 10.
  2. Ask the practical questions. Do you need experience? Is equipment provided? What physical or clearance forms are required?
  3. Plan for practice time. As one example, Seabury Hall's teams practiced Monday–Friday, 4–6 p.m., at the Seabury Hall Air Riflery Range (above the CAC) in 2026. Other schools set their own schedules.

Younger keiki and community clubs

We couldn't find a community or 4-H air rifle club for younger keiki in Maui County right now. If one exists, please tell us so we can share it with families.

Parent FAQ

Is air riflery safe?

It's run by trained coaches under strict range safety rules set by the schools, the MIL and the HHSAA, and students only shoot on supervised school ranges.

Which Maui schools have air riflery teams?

For 2026, Baldwin, Maui High, King Kekaulike, Kamehameha Schools Maui, Seabury Hall, Kūlanihākoʻi, Hāna, Molokaʻi High and Maui Christian Academy have teams.

When is the season?

It's a fall sport. In 2026, MIL matches ran from Sept. 5 to the Oct. 17 MIL Championships, and state was Nov. 3 on Oʻahu.

Does my teen need experience or their own equipment?

Ask your school's coach. Equipment and beginner policies are set by each school, and team details are on each school's athletics site.

Can girls and boys both compete?

Yes. The MIL runs separate boys' and girls' competition, and most schools field both.

Is there air riflery for middle schoolers?

We haven't found one in Maui County yet. Air riflery here is a high school sport.
