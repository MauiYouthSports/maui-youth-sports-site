Focus. Discipline. Teamwork

Youth air riflery in Maui County is primarily a supervised school sport, with boys and girls teams on Maui and Molokaʻi. School-hosted and postal matches form the fall MIL pathway. Coaches manage range access, safety and equipment.

Start with the right pathway

Ask your school coach about team eligibility and supervised practice.

Youth Pathways

School teams are the established local pathway. See the school programs below.

School Programs

School teams are open to enrolled students. The athletics office can help with tryouts, clearance, equipment and practices.
