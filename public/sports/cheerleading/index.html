Cheerleading

More Than Spirit. Strength in Every Move

Cheer builds trust, timing and teamwork, from sideline squads to competitive routines.

Cheerleading combines coordinated movement, jumps, tumbling, stunts, dance and sideline leadership. Depending on the program, young athletes may focus on supporting a football team, preparing competition routines, or both. Safe technique, trust and teamwork are especially important as athletes progress into partner stunts and more advanced skills.

Maui youth cheer programs include community and school-based opportunities.
