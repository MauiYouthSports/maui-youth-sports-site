Every Lap Is Progress

Swimming and diving build water confidence, technique and determination.

Competitive swimming is built around efficient technique, starts, turns and racing across different strokes and distances. Diving is a separate discipline that combines approach, takeoff, body control and entry into the water. Both reward repetition and precise technique, but the skills and competition formats are very different.

Maui has year-round aquatic programs as well as school competition.
