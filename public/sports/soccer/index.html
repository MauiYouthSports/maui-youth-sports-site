One Ball. Endless Possibilities

From neighborhood fields to school competition, soccer connects players across Maui County.

Soccer is a continuous team game built around passing, dribbling, shooting, defending and creating space without the ball. Young players can begin with small-sided games that maximize touches before moving into larger fields, specialized positions and more tactical team play. The sport develops endurance, footwork and quick decision-making.

Maui offers recreational, club and school soccer for a wide range of ages and skill levels.
