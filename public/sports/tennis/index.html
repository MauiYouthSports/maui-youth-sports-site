One Court. So Many Ways to Grow

Tennis develops movement, strategy and resilience, one rally at a time.

Tennis combines serving, groundstrokes, volleys, movement and point-by-point strategy. Young players often begin on smaller courts with age-appropriate balls before progressing to full-court singles and doubles. The sport develops coordination, footwork and the ability to solve problems independently during competition.

Maui youth tennis includes instructional programs, tournaments and school competition.
