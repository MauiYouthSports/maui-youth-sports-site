One Swing Can Start a Lifelong Game

Golf invites patience, practice and personal growth from the first lesson onward.

Golf challenges players to move a ball from the tee into the hole in as few strokes as possible, using different clubs for distance, accuracy and short-game control. Young golfers learn swing fundamentals, putting, course management, rules and etiquette. The sport rewards patience, honesty and the ability to recover mentally from a poor shot.

Maui’s golf courses give junior players unusual access to a sport deeply connected to the island’s recreation and visitor economy.
