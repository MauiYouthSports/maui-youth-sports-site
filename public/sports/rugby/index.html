Rugby

Run Together. Rise Together

Rugby is built on support, courage and a team that moves as one.

Rugby is a continuous team sport built around running, passing, kicking, tackling and support play. The ball is passed laterally or backward by hand, so players must move together and create space as a team. Youth rugby introduces the game progressively, with age-appropriate rules and an emphasis on safe technique, sportsmanship and decision-making.

Maui has a strong community rugby tradition with opportunities for young players in several areas of the island.
