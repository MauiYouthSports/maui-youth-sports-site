Read the Ocean. Ride Your Own Wave

Surfing pairs ocean awareness, patience and confidence with a deep respect for the water.

Competitive surfing asks athletes to read changing ocean conditions, select waves and perform maneuvers with speed, control and commitment. Unlike sports played on a fixed field, every heat is shaped by the ocean. Young surfers also need strong swimming ability, ocean awareness, etiquette and respect for other water users.

Surfing is a natural part of Maui youth athletics, from amateur contests to school competition.
