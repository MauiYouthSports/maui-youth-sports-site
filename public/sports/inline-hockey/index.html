Keep Moving. Keep the Puck Alive

Inline hockey brings speed, coordination and teamwork to the rink.

Inline hockey brings the speed and stick skills of hockey to a hard playing surface using wheeled skates instead of ice skates. Players learn skating, puck control, passing, shooting, positioning and team defense. Because movement happens continuously, balance and skating confidence are a major part of a young player’s development.

Maui has an established inline-hockey community centered at the Kalama Park rink in Kīhei.
