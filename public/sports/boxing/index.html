Maui Youth Boxing

Last checked Nov 2026

Maui keiki can learn boxing at two long-running nonprofit clubs: Central Maui Boxing Club in Kahului and Southside Boxing Club of Maui in Kīhei. Both clubs teach fundamentals, discipline and respect first. Competing in sanctioned amateur bouts is a separate step that comes later, and only when coach, athlete and ʻohana agree a boxer is ready.

What youth boxing is (and isn't)

A good youth boxing gym feels a lot like any other team practice: warm-ups, footwork, conditioning, bag and pad work, and plenty of coaching on how to carry yourself. For many Maui kids it's also a safe, structured place to be after school, with coaches who double as mentors.

Training and competing are different things. Plenty of kids box for fitness and confidence and never step into a ring for a bout. Families should talk with the coach about sparring, readiness and safety before any of that starts.

Boxing isn't an MIL or HHSAA school sport, so community clubs are the pathway.

Central Maui Boxing Club — Kahului

Founded in 1993, Central Maui Boxing Club (CMBC) has long served Maui youth, especially kids who are at risk or facing money challenges. Its gym is at 261 Lalo St., Unit F2, in Kahului.

  • Kids Class, ages 8–12: Monday–Thursday, 3:30–4:30 p.m.
  • Youth/Advanced/Competition Team, ages 13–17: Monday–Friday, 4–6:15 p.m. This is CMBC's USA Boxing competitive team, with inter-island and off-island travel.
  • Contact: Chazz Moleta, 808-276-2362 · centralmauiboxingclub@gmail.com · centralmauiboxingclub.org

CMBC trains both boys and girls, and the club also hosts USA Boxing–sanctioned amateur shows on Maui.

Southside Boxing Club of Maui — Kīhei

Southside Boxing Club of Maui (SSBC) has trained Maui kids since 1996 under head coach Nante Manangan. The club says it welcomes kids from 6 years old, and that as many as 40 students can attend an after-school class. Its mission is simple: give Maui youth a positive place to go and build discipline through boxing.

SSBC is a donation-supported 501(c)(3), backed by its coach, supporters and fundraisers. Reach out before your first visit to ask about youth class times.

  • Contact: through southsideboxingclubmaui.com

Both Maui clubs had boxers win medals at the 2026 USA Boxing Junior Olympics, proof of how far a keiki can go when they stick with it.

How to get started

  1. Call or message the club first. Ask about the right class for your child's age, practice times and whether there's a trial session.
  2. Ask the safety questions. When does sparring begin? What protective gear is required, and what does the club provide? Is a physical or medical clearance needed?
  3. First visit: bring a water bottle and comfortable workout clothes and shoes, and plan to stay and watch. Ask the coach what gear your keiki will need (hand wraps, gloves, mouthguard, headgear) before you buy anything.
  4. Show up consistently. Coaches look for effort and attitude long before talent.

Registration and current rates

Sign-up details and current rates are on each club's own website, or just ask the coach. If paying for a sport is a worry, talk with the club, and check our scholarships and assistance page.

Girls in the gym

Boxing on Maui isn't just for boys. CMBC trains girls and boys together, and Maui girls have earned national medals. If your daughter is curious, ask the coach whether the club runs coed sessions or a dedicated girls' group.

Competing: the amateur pathway

Boxers who want to compete register with USA Boxing through their club and start with local sanctioned shows. CMBC hosted Valley Isle Showdown #3 at T.U.F. Fieldhouse in Kahului on June 6, 2026. From there, athletes can work toward state, regional and national events, including the USA Boxing Junior Olympics. Off-island travel is a big lift for nonprofit clubs, which is why club fundraisers matter so much.

Molokaʻi and Lānaʻi

Molokaʻi has a proud youth boxing history, and Lānaʻi has offered youth classes in the past. We couldn't confirm a current youth program on either island. If you know of one, please tell us.

Parent FAQ

What age can kids start boxing on Maui?

CMBC's Kids Class is for ages 8–12, and its youth team is for ages 13–17. Southside says it trains kids from age 6.

Do beginners have to spar?

No. Training and sparring are separate steps. Ask the coach how and when sparring is introduced at their club.

How do we sign up?

Contact the club first, ask about the right class for your child's age, and plan a first visit. Registration details are on each club's website.

What happens at a typical practice?

Expect warm-ups, footwork, conditioning, bag and pad work, and coaching on technique and attitude. Sparring comes later, if at all, and only when the coach says a boxer is ready.

Is boxing safe for kids?

Coached, supervised training with proper gear is the foundation. Talk with the coach about safety rules, and read our parents' guide to concussions.

Is boxing a school sport here?

Not in the MIL. Maui keiki box through community clubs like CMBC and Southside.
