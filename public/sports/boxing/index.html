Central Maui Boxing Club

Reach out to the club to find the right age group for your child.

A nonprofit youth-development club founded in 1993, building discipline, respect and confidence through coached boxing. The club hosts sanctioned amateur competition; its athlete Faith Kahula Boteilho earned national bronze in 2026.

Molokaʻi and Lānaʻi

Historical Molokaʻi clubs and Lānaʻi Community Health Center classes are not listed as current programs. Ask local recreation or health-center staff about current opportunities; old schedules and age ranges are not reused.

Participation

Contact the club before visiting to ask about age requirements, registration, training location and practice schedules. Ask about protective equipment, medical requirements and sparring policies. No current Molokaʻi or Lānaʻi boxing program is listed here.
