Maui Youth Gymnastics

Last checked Nov 2026

Maui keiki have three main ways into gymnastics: two Kahului gyms, 808 Gymnastics and Island Athletics Maui, and Maui Tumblers, a mobile program that brings gymnastics to schools across Maui and, as of 2026, Molokaʻi. Classes start with toddlers and a parent, and go up through school-age tumbling and, at 808 Gymnastics, a competitive team.

Why gymnastics is great for keiki

Gymnastics builds strength, balance, coordination and body awareness, the foundation for almost every other sport. Just as important, kids learn to try something hard, fall safely and try again.

Class levels, explained

  • Parent & tot (toddlers): a parent or caregiver on the floor, with simple movement and play on real equipment.
  • Preschool: kids who are ready to join class without a parent on the floor. They work on basic skills, coordination and listening.
  • Recreational (school-age): an introduction to the women's and men's events, taught step by step with an emphasis on safe progressions.
  • Tumbling and ninja: floor skills like cartwheels, round-offs, handsprings and flips. These are useful for cheer and dance kids too.
  • Competitive team: structured training and USA Gymnastics meets for athletes who are placed on a team by the coaches.

Where to find gymnastics on Maui

808 Gymnastics — Kahului

65 W. Kaʻahumanu Ave., Kahului · (808) 298-9352 · 808gymnastics@gmail.com · 808gymnastics.com

808 Gymnastics offers Mommy & Me for toddlers, preschool classes (ages 3–4 and 4–5), artistic gymnastics (ages 5–7 and 8–12, with girls, boys and coed classes), beginner-to-advanced tumbling, open gym and camps. It's also the clearest competitive pathway in Maui County, with athletes competing in USA Gymnastics meets in 2026. Families interested in team can ask the gym about placement.

Preschool classes are coed, and children under 5 need a parent or caregiver present for the whole class. Friday-evening open gym is for enrolled students age 5 and up. Current classes are listed in the gym's parent portal.

Island Athletics Maui — Kahului

250 Alamaha St., Unit N12, Kahului · (808) 385-4066 · islandathleticsmaui@gmail.com · registration portal

A year-round recreational gym that moved into a larger Kahului space in 2026. It offers parent & tot, preschool movement, recreational gymnastics, tumbling, ninja tumbling and seasonal camps, with programs starting at age 1.

Maui Tumblers — mobile, Maui and Molokaʻi

(808) 463-5989 · mauitumblers.com

Locally owned since 2004, Maui Tumblers brings the equipment to your keiki's school or preschool for recreational, non-competitive classes. Some sites are open to the public: on Maui that includes Puʻu Kukui, Makawao, Kula, Pukalani and Princess Nāhiʻenaʻena elementary schools and Kīhei Dance Connection. Other sites are for that school's students only. On Molokaʻi, classes run at Kaunakakai, Kilohana and Maunaloa elementaries, Kualapuʻu Charter School and Nā Kamaliʻi Hoaloha Preschool.

Elementary classes on Maui are usually split into a beginner level (grades K–2) and a mixed level (grades 2–5), held right after school. Families register online, under the child's name.

How to get started

  1. Pick the right level. Go by age and comfort, not by what older kids are doing.
  2. Ask about a trial or open house before committing to a month.
  3. Dress for safety. At 808 Gymnastics, for example, gymnasts wear a leotard, unitard or fitted athletic shorts and shirt. That means no zippers, buttons or jewelry, bare feet, and long hair tied back. Other programs may have their own rules, so ask.
  4. Bring water and plan to arrive a few minutes early for the first class.

Camps and school breaks

808 Gymnastics runs half-day and full-day camps for ages 4 and up, with no experience needed. Campers bring a lunch, snacks and a water bottle. Island Athletics Maui and Maui Tumblers also run seasonal camps and skill clinics. Watch our events page for dates.

Parent FAQ

What age can my child start gymnastics on Maui?

Toddler classes with a parent start around age 1 to 3, depending on the gym. Preschool classes start at about age 3.

What's the difference between recreational and competitive gymnastics?

Recreational classes are for fun, fitness and skills at your child's own pace. Competitive team athletes train more hours and compete in USA Gymnastics meets. On Maui, 808 Gymnastics offers the competitive pathway.

How do we sign up?

Each program registers families on its own website, where you'll find current classes and rates. Maui Tumblers and 808 Gymnastics both take registration online.

Are there classes outside Kahului?

Yes. Maui Tumblers teaches at schools in Kīhei, Central, West and Upcountry Maui and on Molokaʻi, and several of those sites are open to the public.

What should my child wear?

Fitted athletic clothes or a leotard, bare feet, hair tied back and no jewelry. Check your gym's dress code.

My child does cheer or dance. Is tumbling worth it?

Tumbling classes focus on the floor skills cheer and dance routines use, and Maui Tumblers has run skill clinics aimed at cheerleaders. Ask the gym which level fits.
