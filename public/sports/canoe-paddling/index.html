Canoe Paddling

Organization Directory →

In a waʻa, every seat matters. Youth paddling builds rhythm, endurance and trust while connecting keiki with Hawaiʻi’s ocean culture. Across Maui County, community clubs, school teams and cultural programs offer different ways to find that connection.

Start with your island and your child’s age. Summer club racing and winter school paddling are distinct pathways. Contact the club for youth program availability.

High School Paddling

MIL / HHSAA pathway

A winter season, a school team

MIL paddling generally runs November–February, separate from summer community-club regattas. The 2025–26 season included Division I and Division II competition. The schools below participated or listed paddling in 2025–26. Contact school athletics for 2026–27 teams and tryout information.

Before school practice

Maui High lists a current physical, parent consent and a swim test, with practice beginning near the end of November. Ask your own school’s athletics office for its requirements, eligibility and meeting location.
