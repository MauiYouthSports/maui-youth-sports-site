One Touch Sets Up the Next

Volleyball rewards communication, quick thinking and teammates working in rhythm.

Volleyball is a fast team sport built around serving, passing, setting, attacking, blocking and court defense. Because teams are limited to three contacts before returning the ball, players learn to communicate and rely on one another on nearly every rally. Younger athletes can start with fundamentals before moving into specialized positions and faster offensive systems.

Maui has active club, recreational and school volleyball for boys and girls.
