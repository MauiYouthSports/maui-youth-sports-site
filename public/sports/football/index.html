One Team. One ʻOhana. Every Yard Earned

From community football to MIL Friday nights, the game is built on preparation and teamwork.

Tackle football is a highly structured team sport that combines running, passing, blocking, tackling and specialized positions on offense, defense and special teams. Youth players learn technique, assignments, conditioning and teamwork while gradually taking on more complex playbooks and position responsibilities. Proper coaching, equipment fit and safe tackling fundamentals are essential parts of the sport.

Maui youth football includes community programs and school competition.
