Every Mile Begins Together

Endurance grows step by step, with teammates and trails that make each finish meaningful.

Cross country is distance running over outdoor courses that may include grass, dirt, hills and changing terrain. Unlike track, where runners compete on a measured oval, cross-country courses vary from meet to meet. Athletes build endurance, pacing, strength and the mental discipline to manage a sustained effort over distance.

On Maui, cross country is especially visible through school competition, with courses shaped by the island’s varied terrain and weather.
