Water Polo

ScoresScheduleFind a TeamRegistrationHighlightsMYS ArchiveSchool Programs

Strong Swimmers. Stronger Teamwork

Water polo combines endurance, skill and split-second decisions in the pool.

Water polo combines swimming, passing, shooting and team defense in a game played entirely in the pool. Players tread water throughout play and must control the ball with one hand, making swimming strength and endurance essential. Young athletes typically build strong swim skills first, then learn positioning, passing and game tactics.

Maui youth water polo grows out of the island’s broader aquatics community and school competition.

What families should know

The established school pathway is girls varsity water polo. Ask school coaches about swimming prerequisites and tryouts. A pool listing alone does not establish open age-group club enrollment.
