Find Your Rhythm. Make It Yours

Dance brings movement, expression and community together across styles and ages.

Dance develops balance, coordination, rhythm, flexibility, strength and performance skills. Youth programs can range from recreational instruction to teams and competitions, with styles that may include jazz, hip-hop, contemporary, ballet and culturally rooted forms. The emphasis can vary widely, so families should look at both the style and the goals of each program.

Maui has a broad dance community with opportunities for young performers at different experience levels.
