From the First Swing to the Final Out

Little fields. Big dreams. A game shared across generations.

From first-time T-ball players to MIL varsity athletes, Maui County baseball offers community leagues, development programs and school teams across Maui, Molokaʻi and Lānaʻi. Explore the pathways below and check each organization for current eligibility and registration.

Little League, PONY and school pathways

Five Little League programs competed in District III in 2026: Central East Maui, Upcountry, Kīhei, West Maui and Molokaʻi. Lānaʻi Little League is listed in the County directory, so contact the league about this season. Maui PONY 13–14 participation also requires confirmation.

Dates and contacts can change, so check with the league before you register.
