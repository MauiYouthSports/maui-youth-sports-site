Martial Arts

ScoresScheduleFind a TeamRegistrationHighlightsMYS Archive

Respect First. Confidence Follows

Martial arts offers many paths to discipline, fitness and personal growth.

Martial arts is an umbrella for disciplines that develop movement, technique, control and self-discipline. Maui programs may teach striking arts, grappling arts, or a combination of both, and competition rules differ greatly among judo, Brazilian jiu-jitsu, karate, taekwondo, boxing, kickboxing and related disciplines.

Martial arts disciplines differ in their skills and training. Ask each program which martial art it teaches and which classes suit your child’s age and experience.
