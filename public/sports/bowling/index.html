Every Frame Is a Fresh Start

From youth instruction to MIL lanes, bowling makes room for skill, focus and friendly competition.

Competitive bowling turns a familiar recreational activity into a precision sport. Players learn a repeatable approach, ball release, lane targeting, spare shooting and how changing lane conditions affect the path of the ball. Youth competition also teaches scoring, concentration and the ability to make small adjustments from frame to frame.

Maui youth bowlers can compete through organized programs and school competition.
