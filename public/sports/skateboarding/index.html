Skateboarding

ScoresScheduleFind a TeamRegistrationHighlightsMYS Archive

Find Your Line. Keep Trying

From the first push to the next trick, progress comes through practice and persistence.

Skateboarding combines balance, coordination, creativity and repeated skill progression. Young skaters learn board control, pushing, turning and stopping before progressing to ramps, transitions, grinds and tricks. Organized skate events can include lessons, jams and contests divided by age or ability.

Maui has public skate parks and youth-oriented skate events.
