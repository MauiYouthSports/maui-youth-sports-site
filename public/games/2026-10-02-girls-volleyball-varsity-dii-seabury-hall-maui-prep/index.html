Seabury keeps its winning run going

Seabury Hall and Maui Prep arrived at their October 2 match with winning streaks, but only the Spartans’ continued. Seabury’s 3–0 victory was its ninth consecutive win and brought its reported overall record to 11–2. Maui Prep’s three-match winning run ended with the Pueo at 5–10.

The published player numbers show contributions on both sides of the net. Maui Prep’s Zoey Offergeld recorded nine kills and seven digs, while Ashley Davis added 13 assists and five digs. For Seabury, Milaniakai Padilla finished with 10 kills and four service aces, and Destiny Keomaka-Wood supplied 29 assists.

Those individual totals add detail to the straight-set result without establishing how each set unfolded. Set scores and point-by-point play were not available in the sources reviewed for this MYS recap.
