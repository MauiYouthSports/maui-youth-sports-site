Privacy Policy

Maui Youth Sports collects information you choose to send through our contact and submission forms, such as your name, email address, message, sports information, and optional phone number. We use this information to review submissions, answer inquiries, verify information, and administer the website. Submitted materials are not published automatically. Approved sports content may be published publicly, including on related MYS social media. Do not submit sensitive personal information about children. Contact us using the Contact page to request access, correction, or removal of material. We may retain records needed to document permissions, address disputes, or protect site integrity. Cloudflare hosts the website. Formspree processes form messages and sends notifications to MYS. Photo and video uploads are not currently offered. Do not submit passwords or other confidential information. This draft must be reconciled with the actual production backend, retention practices, analytics, and any third-party services before launch.
