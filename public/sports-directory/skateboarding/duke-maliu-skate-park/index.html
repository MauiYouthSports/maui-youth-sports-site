Skateboarding / Duke Maliu Skate Park

Molokaʻi · Kaunakakai

Duke Maliu Skate Park

A County skatepark with wooden ramps, platforms, stairs and rails. Park access does not include an organized lesson or youth league.

Program & participation

Where
90 Ainoa St., Kaunakakai
Ages / eligibility
Public recreation; supervision is the family’s responsibility
Season / sessions
Open daily 7 a.m.–10 p.m. (County hours).
Joining
Free public park. Bring a helmet and pads, and check County park rules before you go.
