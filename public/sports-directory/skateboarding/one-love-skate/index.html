Skateboarding / One Love Skate

Maui · Pukalani / Pāʻia

One Love Skate

Faith-based skateboarding instruction and mentorship through weekly Acts 4:20 groups, community partnerships, camps and skate jams. Coached groups are distinct from independent use of public skateparks.

Program & participation

Where
Pukalani Skatepark and Pāʻia Youth & Cultural Center.
Ages / eligibility
Contact the program about ages, waivers and group size.
Season / sessions
Pukalani on Thursdays: gather at 4:20 p.m., practice 4:30–5:30 p.m. A Pāʻia group also meets Thursdays; contact the organizers for details.
Joining
Contact the coaches before your first session about dates, ages, waivers, equipment and openings.
Participation notes
Kīhei, Hāna, Lahaina and Keōpūolani are not active weekly One Love groups. Pop-up events at those parks are separate. The September 26, 2026 Pukalani Skate Jam was suspended; a new date hasn’t been announced.
