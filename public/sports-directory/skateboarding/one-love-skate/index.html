Skateboarding / One Love Skate

Maui · Pukalani / Pāʻia

One Love Skate

Faith-based skateboarding instruction and mentorship through weekly Acts 4:20 groups, community partnerships, camps and skate jams. Coached groups are distinct from independent use of public skateparks.

Program & participation

Where
Pukalani Skatepark and Pāʻia Youth & Cultural Center.
Ages / eligibility
Ask about ages, waiver and group capacity
Season / sessions
Pukalani Thursdays: 4:20 p.m. gathering, 4:30–5:30 p.m. practice. Pāʻia Thursday group: confirm with organizers.
Joining
Contact the coaches before attending. Ask about current dates, ages, waivers, equipment and spaces.
Participation notes
Kīhei, Hāna, Lahaina and Keōpūolani are not active weekly One Love groups. Pop-up events at those parks are separate. The September 26, 2026 Pukalani Skate Jam was suspended; a replacement date is not confirmed here.
