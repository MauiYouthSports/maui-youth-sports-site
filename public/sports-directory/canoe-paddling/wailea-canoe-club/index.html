Structured youth age groups sit within a club serving young paddlers, their families and the wider community.

Program at a glance

Ages / eligibility
Kamaliʻi 8 & under · 9–13 · 14+
Community / practice location
South Maui; contact the club for the youth meeting point.
Opportunities
Competitive and recreational paddling; MCHCA and HCRA youth competition.
Season
2026 summer-regatta practices began May 4. Contact the club for current practices and next-season dates.
