Canoe Paddling / Maui
Youth program confirmed

Central Maui · Kahului

Nā Kai ʻEwalu

Paddling, mentoring and Hawaiian cultural education come together in a club that fields crews from young paddlers through kūpuna.

Program at a glance

Ages / eligibility
Crews begin at age 9
Community / practice location
Hoaloha Park, Kahului Harbor
Opportunities
MCHCA regatta competition, distance paddling and youth mentoring; eligibility for specific distance crews should be confirmed.
Season
Regatta season April–August; distance season August–October. Confirm current youth practices.

How to join

Contact the club for youth registration information. Ask about fees, swimming requirements and practice schedules.

The club explicitly hosts Baldwin High School and Kamehameha Schools Maui during the MIL season. This relationship does not establish practice arrangements for other schools.
