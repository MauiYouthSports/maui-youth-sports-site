Canoe Paddling / Maui
Youth program confirmed

Central Maui · Kahului

Nā Kai ʻEwalu

Paddling, mentoring and Hawaiian cultural education come together in a club that fields crews from young paddlers through kūpuna.

Program at a glance

Ages / eligibility
Crews begin at age 9
Community / practice location
Hoaloha Park, Kahului Harbor
Opportunities
MCHCA regatta competition, distance paddling and youth mentoring. Contact the club about distance crews.
Season
Regatta season runs April–August and distance season August–October. Contact the club for youth practice times.

How to join

Contact the club to sign up. They can tell you about fees, the swim requirement and practice times.

The club explicitly hosts Baldwin High School and Kamehameha Schools Maui during the MIL season. This relationship does not establish practice arrangements for other schools.
