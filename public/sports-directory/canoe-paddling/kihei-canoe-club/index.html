Canoe Paddling / Maui
Youth program confirmed

South Maui · North Kīhei

Kīhei Canoe Club

A junior racing pathway, plus a Saturday Keiki Paddling Program that continues beyond the summer regatta season.

Program at a glance

Ages / eligibility
Manini 8–9 · Juniors 10–18
Community / practice location
North Kīhei. Message the club for the exact meeting spot.
Opportunities
Youth racing with Saturday MCHCA regattas and an HCRA state-championship pathway; separate recurring keiki sessions.
Season
Youth racing ran April 6–August 1, 2026. Keiki sessions continue into fall 2026.

How to join

Contact the club to sign up. 2026 racing dues were $60, which included a race jersey, and paddles were provided.

2026 racing practices were afternoons in April–May and mornings in June–July. The October 10 keiki listing runs 9–11 a.m., is categorized Member Youth Program and says registration is not required; ask about membership and first-time participation before attending.
