Youth program confirmed

Central Maui · Kahului

Hawaiian Canoe Club

A community rooted in waʻa, teamwork and Hawaiian culture, with age-group racing and Kamaliʻi enrichment. Older youth can also grow through the Youth Leadership Program.

Program at a glance

Ages / eligibility
8 & under · 10–13 · 14+
Community / practice location
Hoaloha Park, Kahului Harbor · 1 E. Kaʻahumanu Ave.
Opportunities
Youth regatta racing, cultural enrichment and leadership for ages 14–18.
Season
2026 youth practices began May 4. Contact the club for practice times and next season’s dates.

How to join

Contact the club to sign up. They’ll help you find the right age group, practice times and dues.

The published age groups are reproduced as listed; families of nine-year-olds should ask the club which group applies.
