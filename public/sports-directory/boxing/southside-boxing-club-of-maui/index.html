Southside Boxing Club of Maui

A community nonprofit led by Nante Manangan, pairing boxing fundamentals with mentoring and character development. Southside boxer Micah Diaz won a 2026 Junior Olympics national title.

Where
Kīhei. Contact the club for the training location.

Joining

Participation notes
The club describes a donation-supported free youth mission. Ask which training, equipment and travel costs are currently covered; assistance is not a guarantee of a place or a universally free package.
