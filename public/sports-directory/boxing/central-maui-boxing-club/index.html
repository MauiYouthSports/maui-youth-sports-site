Maui · Central Maui

Central Maui Boxing Club

A nonprofit youth-development club founded in 1993, building discipline, respect and confidence through coached boxing. The club hosts sanctioned amateur competition; its athlete Faith Kahula Boteilho earned national bronze in 2026.

Program & participation

Where
Reach out to the club for the gym address and training times. Competition bouts are held at separate venues.
Ages / eligibility
Reach out to the club to find the right age group for your child.
Season / sessions
The club trains year-round. Contact the coach for this season’s schedule.
Joining
Contact the program to sign up. They can tell you about age groups, openings, fees and practice times.
Participation notes
Ask about beginner instruction, protective gear, medical requirements and readiness for sparring. A sanctioned bout requires the club’s approval and USA Boxing participation requirements.
