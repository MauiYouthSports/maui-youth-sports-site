Wrestling / Nakamura Wrestling

Maui · Wailuku

Nakamura Wrestling

Youth club training, private instruction and college-pathway development. The club won the 2026 Hawaiʻi USA Wrestling Folkstyle Championship.

Program & participation

Where
1552 Mill St., Wailuku
Ages / eligibility
Youth athletes. Contact the program for age groups.
Season / sessions
Published hours Mon–Thu 3:45–5:30 p.m.; Sun 2:45–5:30 p.m.; Fri by appointment.
Joining
Contact the program to sign up. They can tell you about age groups, openings, fees and practice times.
Participation notes
Ask about beginner placement, monthly dues and USA Wrestling membership. Published one-hour private/small-group lessons: members $120, nonmembers $150, maximum four.
