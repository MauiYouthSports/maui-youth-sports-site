Swimming & Diving / Maui Dolphins Swim Club

Countywide · Pukalani / Molokaʻi / Lānaʻi

Maui Dolphins Swim Club

Intro to Swim Team and year-round competitive swimming, with published competitive practice operations on all three islands. Neighbor-island groups have separate local arrangements.

Program & participation

Where
Upcountry Aquatic Center, 90 Pukalani St. Contact the program for Molokaʻi and Lānaʻi locations.
Ages / eligibility
Intro typically 5–10 with swimming prerequisites
Season / sessions
Short course September–February; long course March–August.
Joining
Competitive team enrollment is closed for now. Intro sessions run every so often, usually 6–8 weeks. Contact the club about the next one.
Participation notes
Intro swimmers must be comfortable in 3.5–4 feet, swim one length and submerge/blow bubbles. Published competitive dues $600/season, additional sibling $500, 2026 USA Swimming $90. Prices can change, so check with the program.
