Countywide · Pukalani / Molokaʻi / Lānaʻi

Maui Dolphins Swim Club

Intro to Swim Team and year-round competitive swimming, with published competitive practice operations on all three islands. Neighbor-island groups have separate local arrangements.

Program & participation

Where
Upcountry Aquatic Center, 90 Pukalani St.; ask for Molokaʻi/Lānaʻi meeting points.
Ages / eligibility
Intro typically 5–10 with swimming prerequisites
Season / sessions
Short course September–February; long course March–August.
Joining
Competitive enrollment was listed closed; ask about the next assessment/announcement. Intro sessions run periodically, usually 6–8 weeks.
Participation notes
Intro swimmers must be comfortable in 3.5–4 feet, swim one length and submerge/blow bubbles. Published competitive dues $600/season, additional sibling $500, 2026 USA Swimming $90; confirm next-season rates.
