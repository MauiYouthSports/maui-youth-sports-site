Maui · Kahului

Maui Family YMCA Aquatics

Progressive water comfort, safety and stroke lessons, private instruction and a noncompetitive Youth Swim Club emphasizing technique, speed and endurance.

Program & participation

Where
250 Kanaloa Ave., Kahului
Ages / eligibility
Lessons 6 months+ · youth swim club 5+ with prerequisites
Season / sessions
Lessons run in two-month sessions. Contact the Y to sign up for the next one.
Joining
Contact the program to sign up. They can tell you about age groups, openings, fees and practice times.
Participation notes
Youth Swim Club requires Level 3 stamina and a 50-yard lap of front and back crawl. Published private package: six 45-minute lessons $147 member/$294 nonmember; learn-to-swim four 30-minute lessons $89/$178. Prices can change, so check with the program.
