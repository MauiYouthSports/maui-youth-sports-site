Maui · Kahului

Healthy Kids Running Series Maui

Five weekly age-appropriate running events from short dashes through one mile, with a Challenger Division. This is a beginner running series rather than a school cross-country team or full track-and-field program.

Program & participation

Where
Pōmaikaʻi Elementary, 4650 S. Kamehameha Ave.
Ages / eligibility

Season / sessions
Fall 2026: October 18, 25; November 1, 8, 15, 4–5 p.m.
Joining
