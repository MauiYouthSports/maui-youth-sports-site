Valley Isle Road Runners Youth Program

A community running nonprofit with a youth cross-country series: half-mile K–1 and 2–3, one mile grades 4–5, and 1.5 miles grades 6–8. School teams and unattached runners can participate.

Program & participation

Where
Maui
Ages / eligibility
Youth cross country K–8; student membership through 17
Joining
Contact the club for upcoming series dates. Registration is on Athletic.net.
Participation notes
Youth series participation is described as free; separate keiki/student club membership is $20/year. General adult group runs have separate supervision and age questions.
