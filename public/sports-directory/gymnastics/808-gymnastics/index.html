Gymnastics / 808 Gymnastics

Maui · Kahului

808 Gymnastics

Recreational artistic gymnastics, tumbling and a USA Gymnastics competitive pathway. Classes use floor, bars, beams, vault, tumble track and trampoline; team placement is separate from recreational enrollment.

Program & participation

Where
65 W. Kaʻahumanu Ave., Kahului
Ages / eligibility
Parent–child 1–3 · preschool 3–5 · school-age classes · camps 4+
Season / sessions
Regular classes and seasonal school-break camps.
Joining

Participation notes
Published monthly tuition: $90 parent–child/preschool, $95 one regular class weekly, $155 two weekly; $30 annual administration fee and 10% sibling discount. Confirm the class level and current rate.
