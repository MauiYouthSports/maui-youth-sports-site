Maui · Kahului

808 Gymnastics

Recreational gymnastics, tumbling and a USA Gymnastics competitive team. Classes use floor, bars, beam, vault, tumble track and trampoline.

Program & participation

Where
65 W. Kaʻahumanu Ave., Kahului
Ages / eligibility
Parent–child 1–3 · preschool 3–5 · school-age classes · camps 4+
Season / sessions
Regular classes and seasonal school-break camps.
Joining
Contact the program to sign up. They can tell you about age groups, openings, fees and practice times.
Participation notes
Published monthly tuition: $90 parent–child/preschool, $95 one regular class weekly, $155 two weekly; $30 annual administration fee and 10% sibling discount. Prices can change, so check with the program.
