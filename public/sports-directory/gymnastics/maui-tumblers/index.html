Maui Tumblers

Mobile, noncompetitive gymnastics with after-school classes, private and small-group lessons, and skill workshops. Some classes are held at schools for their students.

Program & participation

Where
Maui and Molokaʻi
Ages / eligibility
Preschool–elementary; selected sites through grade 8
Season / sessions
2026–27 school-year classes.
Joining
Contact the program to sign up. They can tell you about age groups, openings, fees and practice times.
Participation notes
Maui public-site offerings include Makawao, Kula and Pukalani Elementary, Haʻikū community classes and Seabury grades 3–8. Carden, Montessori and Nā Pua O Kula classes are student-only. Molokaʻi offerings include Kaunakakai and Kilohana Elementary and Nā Kamaliʻi Hoaloha Preschool. Published monthly rates: Maui Pre-K $58 + GET / elementary $75 + GET; Molokaʻi $55 / $72; sibling discount 10%.
