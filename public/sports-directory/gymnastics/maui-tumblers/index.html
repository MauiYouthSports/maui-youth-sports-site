Gymnastics / Maui Tumblers

Countywide · Maui and Molokaʻi

Maui Tumblers

Mobile, noncompetitive gymnastics and movement with after-school classes, private/small-group instruction and skill workshops. School-hosted classes differ in public access; confirm the site’s eligibility.

Program & participation

Where
Maui and Molokaʻi
Ages / eligibility
Preschool–elementary; selected sites through grade 8
Season / sessions
2026–27 school-year classes.
Joining
Contact the program for youth registration information. Ask about age requirements, openings, fees and practice schedules before visiting.
Participation notes
Maui public-site offerings include Makawao, Kula and Pukalani Elementary, Haʻikū community classes and Seabury grades 3–8. Carden, Montessori and Nā Pua O Kula classes are student-only. Molokaʻi offerings include Kaunakakai and Kilohana Elementary and Nā Kamaliʻi Hoaloha Preschool. Published monthly rates: Maui Pre-K $58 + GET / elementary $75 + GET; Molokaʻi $55 / $72; sibling discount 10%.
