Valley Isle Softball Little League

A girls softball league welcoming beginners, with development toward Little League Major and Junior All-Stars. Its first season opened in January 2026 with 17 teams.

Program & participation

Where
Central Maui Regional Sports Complex, Kahului School fields and Papohaku. Your coach will share your team’s field.
Ages / eligibility
Ages 4–16. Contact the program for this season’s divisions.
Season / sessions
The first season began January 17, 2026. Contact the league about the next one.
Joining
Contact the league about registration, age divisions, equipment and fees. Need-based scholarships have been offered.
Participation notes
