Inline Hockey / Maui Inline Hockey Association

Maui · Kīhei

Maui Inline Hockey Association

Inline hockey at Kalama Park with youth practices and league games. Players register with AAU first, then with the league. Financial help is available.

Program & participation

Where
Kalama Park inline rink, 1900 S. Kīhei Rd.
Ages / eligibility
Youth league. Contact the league for this season’s divisions.
Season / sessions
2026–27 registration opened August 15. Youth hockey runs Monday–Thursday at 5 p.m.
Joining
Contact the program to sign up. They can tell you about age groups, openings, fees and practice times.
Participation notes
Public skating Friday/Saturday 5–9 p.m. is general skating, not hockey instruction. Bring your own skates to skate free; rentals are listed at $10. Ask about hockey fees, beginner skills, protective gear and loaners.
