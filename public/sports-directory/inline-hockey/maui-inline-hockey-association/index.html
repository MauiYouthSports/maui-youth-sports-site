Inline Hockey / Maui Inline Hockey Association

Maui · Kīhei

Maui Inline Hockey Association

Organized inline hockey at Kalama Park with youth practices and league play. AAU registration is required for players, coaches and referees before league registration; financial help is available through the association.

Program & participation

Where
Kalama Park inline rink, 1900 S. Kīhei Rd.
Ages / eligibility
Youth league; confirm current divisions
Season / sessions
2026–27 registration opened August 15; youth hockey Monday–Thursday at 5 p.m. Confirm assigned sessions.
Joining

Participation notes
Public skating Friday/Saturday 5–9 p.m. is general skating, not hockey instruction. Bring your own skates to skate free; rentals are listed at $10. Ask about hockey fees, beginner skills, protective gear and loaners.
