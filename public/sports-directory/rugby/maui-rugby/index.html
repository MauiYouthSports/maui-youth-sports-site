Maui Rugby

Maui’s home for youth rugby: recreational and competitive play, Maui Cup sevens, Hawaiʻi Youth Rugby and travel teams. Rookie rugby is noncontact.

Program & participation

Where
Islandwide
Ages / eligibility
Rookie, youth and high-school pathways
Season / sessions
Maui Cup runs January–March, and high-school and travel play runs February–August.
Joining
Contact the program to sign up. They can tell you about age groups, openings, fees and practice times.
Participation notes
USA Rugby/Rugby Xplorer membership is separate from any club dues or subsidies. Do not assume the older 2025–26 registration notice applies to 2026–27.
