Maui Rugby

A nonprofit umbrella organization offering recreational and competitive rugby, Maui Cup sevens, Hawaiʻi Youth Rugby play and travel opportunities. Rookie rugby is noncontact; tackle and high-school formats have separate participation requirements.

Program & participation

Published annual structure: Maui Cup January–March; HYR high-school and travel pathways February–August. Confirm the next season.
